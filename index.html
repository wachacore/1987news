Russian court orders $20.45 million and ₽3.9 billion in damages against Anatoly Chubais and former Rosnano executives

The Moscow Arbitration Court partially granted Rosnano’s claim, ordering the former head of the corporation and six former top managers to pay about ₽3.9 billion and $20.45 million. The lawsuit was filed over losses incurred due to the failed Plastic Logic flexible tablet project.

EU warns of long-term energy shock from Middle East conflict

EU Energy Commissioner Dan Jørgensen stated that Europe must prepare for a prolonged energy crisis due to the war in the Middle East. The bloc is considering measures such as fuel rationing and releasing oil from emergency reserves. Jørgensen warned that energy prices will remain high for a long time, and the situation for some critical products may worsen in the coming weeks.

Turkey proposes alternative energy routes bypassing the Strait of Hormuz

Turkish Trade Minister Omer Bolat announced five alternative transport routes for oil and gas supplies to circumvent the Strait of Hormuz amid tensions around Iran. The options include land deliveries through Iraq and Syria, a combined route via the Suez Canal, the Red Sea, Jordan, and Saudi Arabia, as well as a route from the port of Oman to the UAE and other countries in the region.

Launch of China’s Tianlong-3 rocket ends in failure

The launch of the Chinese heavy liquid-fuel rocket Tianlong-3, which is positioned as an analogue of SpaceX’s Falcon 9, failed. The incident occurred at the Jiuquan Cosmodrome in the Gobi Desert. An anomaly was recorded during the flight, after which the launch was declared unsuccessful. The Space Pioneer company, which developed the rocket, plans to conduct a comprehensive analysis of the situation.

Trump threatens to strike Iran’s bridges and power plants

US President Donald Trump warned about striking and destroying bridges and electric power plants in Iran. In a social media post, he wrote: «The US military hasn’t even started destroying what’s left in Iran. Bridges next, then Electric Power Plants». Trump also stated that Iran’s leadership «knows what has to be done, and has to be done, FAST!».

NASA’s Orion spacecraft begins lunar mission

The Orion spacecraft with a crew of four astronauts successfully performed a manoeuvre to transition from Earth orbit to a lunar trajectory as part of the Artemis II mission. The spacecraft was launched on April 2, 2026, and the mission will last ten days. The crew will perform a flyby of the Moon and then return to Earth.

US Army Chief of Staff Randy George dismissed

Pentagon Secretary Pete Hegseth dismissed US Army Chief of Staff Randy George. The decision was reportedly related to George’s failure to support a ground operation against Iran, which Trump had insisted on. George, the 41st Chief of Staff of the Army, was replaced by Vice Chief of Staff Christopher Lanive, a supporter of Trump.
